# 🎮 About Page Easter Eggs Guide

## Games & Interactive Features

### 1. **Dino Runner Game** 🦖 (MAIN GAME - EASY TO FIND!)
**How to activate:** Click the big **"PLAY DINO RUNNER"** button in the Fun Stats section!
**What happens:** A Chrome Dino-style endless runner game appears!
- Press **SPACE** or **↑** to jump
- Avoid cacti and flying birds
- Score increases as you survive longer
- Speed increases over time
- Press **R** to restart after game over

**Just like the Chrome offline game!** Perfect for when you're bored! 🎮

### 2. **Konami Code - Space Shooter Game** 🚀 (HIDDEN)
**How to activate:** Press the following keys in sequence:
```
↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ ← → ← → B A
```
**What happens:** A secret retro space shooter game appears! 
- Use **Arrow Keys** to move your ship
- Press **Space** to shoot
- Press **R** to restart after game over
- Destroy enemies and avoid their bullets!
- Track your score and health

### 3. **Secret Message** 💬
**How to activate:** Click on the "Espat Karmchari Co-Operative Credit Society" logo **7 times**
**What happens:** A secret congratulations message appears with hints about other easter eggs!

### 4. **Neon Glow Effect** ✨
The company name has a continuous neon pulse animation that makes it glow like a retro arcade sign.

### 5. **Glitch Effect** 🔧
Random glitch effects occur on the company name every few seconds, giving it a cyberpunk aesthetic.

### 6. **Floating Particles** 🌟
20 animated particles float across the screen in the background, creating a dreamy atmosphere.

### 7. **Interactive Feature Cards** 🎯
Hover over the Security, Reliability, Innovation, and Support cards to see:
- Smooth scale and lift animations
- Bouncing icons
- Shadow effects

### 8. **Developer Credit** 👨‍💻
A special animated gradient text shows the developer's name with a rainbow effect.

### 9. **Progress Tracker** 📊
At the bottom of the page, you can see:
- Click counter (for the secret message)
- Konami code progress
- Blinking hint text

### 10. **Hover Effects** 🖱️
- Hover over list items to see them slide
- Hover over stats to see them scale and rotate
- Hover over the main box to see enhanced shadows

### 11. **Animated Border** 🌈
The main content box has a flowing rainbow border that continuously animates around the edges.

## How to Find the Games:

### 🦖 Dino Runner (Easy - Main Game)
1. Scroll down to the "Fun Stats" section
2. Click the big rainbow **"PLAY DINO RUNNER"** button
3. Press SPACE to jump and avoid obstacles!
4. Try to beat your high score!

### 🚀 Space Shooter (Hidden - Easter Egg)
1. Type the Konami Code: ↑↑↓↓←→←→BA
2. Watch the progress tracker at the bottom
3. When complete, the space shooter appears!
4. Use arrows to move and SPACE to shoot!

## Tips for Users:
1. **Start with the Dino Runner** - it's the most fun and easy to find!
2. Try clicking everything - especially the logo 7 times!
3. Look for the progress indicators at the bottom
4. The Konami code is a classic gaming easter egg - try it!
5. Hover over different elements to discover animations
6. The page is fully responsive - try it on mobile!
7. Challenge your friends to beat your high score!

## Game Controls Summary:
- **Dino Runner**: SPACE or ↑ to jump, R to restart
- **Space Shooter**: Arrow keys to move, SPACE to shoot, R to restart
