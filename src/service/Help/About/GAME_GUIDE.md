# 🎮 Quick Game Guide - Where to Find the Games!

## 🦖 DINO RUNNER GAME (Main Game - Super Easy!)

### Where is it?
The game button is **RIGHT THERE** on the About page! You can't miss it!

### Step-by-Step:
1. Open the **Help → About** menu
2. Scroll down to the **"Fun Stats"** section (with the colorful stats)
3. Look for the BIG RAINBOW BUTTON that says **"🦖 PLAY DINO RUNNER 🎮"**
4. Click it!
5. Press **SPACE** to jump!

### Game Features:
- 🦖 Cute T-Rex dinosaur character
- 🌵 Cacti obstacles on the ground
- 🐦 Flying birds in the air
- 📊 Score counter (increases as you survive)
- ⚡ Speed increases over time
- 🎨 Clean, minimalist graphics (just like Chrome!)

### Controls:
- **SPACE** or **↑ Arrow** = Jump
- **R** = Restart after game over

### Tips:
- Time your jumps carefully!
- Watch out for birds - they fly at head height
- The game gets faster as your score increases
- Try to beat your high score!
- Perfect for quick breaks!

## 🚀 SPACE SHOOTER GAME (Hidden Easter Egg)

### Where is it?
This one is HIDDEN! You need to unlock it with a secret code!

### How to Unlock:
Type the **Konami Code** on your keyboard:
```
↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ ← → ← → B A
```

### Progress Tracker:
Look at the bottom of the About page - you'll see:
```
🎯 Easter Eggs: 0/7 clicks | Konami Progress: 0/10
```

As you type the code correctly, the progress will increase!

### Game Features:
- 🚀 Control a spaceship
- 👾 Shoot down enemy ships
- 💥 Avoid enemy bullets
- ❤️ Health system (3 hearts)
- ⭐ Starfield background
- 🎯 Score tracking

### Controls:
- **Arrow Keys** = Move your ship
- **SPACE** = Shoot bullets
- **R** = Restart after game over
